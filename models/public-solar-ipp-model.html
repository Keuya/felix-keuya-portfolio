Workbook architecture

Nine linked sheets with a clear review path

The workbook is deliberately compact enough to inspect while retaining the main mechanics expected in a project-finance review.

01

Cover

Purpose, scope, limitations and colour convention.

02

Dashboard

Investment, debt and model-status outputs.

03

Inputs

Separated technical, commercial, cost, debt, tax and scenario assumptions.

04

Operations

Generation, losses, degradation, revenue and OPEX.

05

Funding

Sources and uses, financing fees, DSRA and debt-cap selection.

06

Debt

Opening balance, interest, principal, DSCR, LLCR and lock-up.

07

Cashflow

CFADS, reserve movement, distributions, IRR and NPV.

08

Sensitivities

Tariff, yield, CAPEX, interest and combined cases.

09

Checks

Funding, debt, covenant and public-disclosure integrity tests.

Live mandate

What changes when this is built for an actual transaction

  • Verified energy study with P50, P75, P90 and, where required, P99 cases.
  • Monthly construction schedule, drawdown, IDC, commitment fees and construction contingencies.
  • Jurisdiction-specific tax, VAT, duties, incentives, loss carryforwards and withholding taxes.
  • Contract-specific tariff, indexation, curtailment, deemed energy, payment delay and termination treatment.
  • Sculpted repayment, cash sweep, reserve waterfalls, lock-up and default covenants.
  • FX, inflation, interest-rate, refinancing and distribution scenarios.
  • Documented model audit trail, issue register and lender or investment-committee memo.
