Scope boundaries

I provide analytical support and clearly state the reliance limits of each assignment.

  • Legal, tax and regulatory opinions remain with licensed advisers.
  • Independent technical engineering opinions remain with appropriately qualified engineers.
  • Investment decisions remain with the client and their authorised decision makers.
  • Missing source information is identified as a gap rather than replaced by an unsupported assumption.
  • Confidential information is not requested through the public form before NDA and scope confirmation.
