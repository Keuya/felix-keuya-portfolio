Decision focus

Market research should change what the project team does next.

I structure market work around practical investment and development questions: Is demand real? Is the tariff credible? Who can buy the power? Is grid capacity available? What procurement route exists? What payment and sovereign risks will lenders care about? Which projects, developers and capital providers are already active?

The output is designed to help a team screen a market, compare countries, prepare an investment case or identify the next diligence questions.

Market screen

What the diligence can cover

Depth depends on the market and decision.

Demand

Power demand and customer base

Demand growth, industrial load, electrification, captive generation and where new renewable supply can realistically be absorbed.

Tariffs

Price and commercial pressure

Utility tariffs, PPA benchmarks, customer savings logic, escalation, affordability and the gap between headline price and bankable revenue.

Grid

Connection and system constraints

Transmission capacity, connection route, curtailment exposure, wheeling access and whether grid delivery can keep pace with generation.

Buyer risk

Offtaker and payment quality

Utility credit, corporate buyer quality, payment security, sovereign support and the risks lenders are likely to price.

Rules

Regulation and procurement

Licensing, IPP frameworks, auctions, bilateral PPAs, wheeling, C&I routes and the practical path from opportunity to contracted project.

Capital

Financing and investor appetite

DFI activity, local lending capacity, guarantee structures, expected leverage, currency issues and comparable transactions.
