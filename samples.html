Solar IPP financial model

What it covers

Public model · Corporate power

South African PPA and wheeling model

ViewsBuyer and generator
CostsPPA, grid and residual supply
QuestionWhat is the delivered saving?

What it covers

  • Generation and customer load matching
  • Network charges, losses and service costs
  • Residual grid purchases and demand charges
  • Buyer savings and generator returns
  • Settlement assumptions and downside cases

The model avoids comparing a PPA tariff with a full grid bill. It rebuilds the delivered cost on both sides of the transaction.
