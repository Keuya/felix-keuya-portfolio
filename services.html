Scope boundaries

Professional analysis with clear reliance limits

  • Legal, tax and regulatory opinions remain with licensed advisers.
  • Independent technical engineering opinions remain with appropriately qualified engineers.
  • Investment decisions remain with the client and authorised decision-makers.
  • Missing evidence is identified as a gap rather than replaced by an unsupported fact.
  • Confidential material is exchanged only after scope and NDA requirements are agreed.
