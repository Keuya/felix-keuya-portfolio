Boundaries

What public samples do and do not prove

  • They demonstrate modelling structure, question framing, quality controls and communication style.
  • They do not disclose client identities, live agreements, proprietary data rooms or confidential transaction formulas.
  • Representative South African and REC cases are not presented as completed client mandates.
  • Public assumptions are not legal, tax, technical or investment advice and must be replaced with project-specific evidence in a live mandate.
  • Live work can be completed under NDA and delivered in the client’s templates and brand.

Apply the method

Send the live project question, current files and deadline.
