Anonymised grid-risk case

Grid connection, wheeling and curtailment review for a 50 MWac solar IPP

A development-stage solar project depended on a proposed substation connection and third-party network use. The investment case therefore needed a clear distinction between modelled export and energy that could actually be delivered and paid for.

01

Situation

The financial model assumed grid export, but final interconnection capacity, network works, metering, dispatch protocols and curtailment treatment remained material dependencies.

02

Mandate

Review the grid and wheeling workstream from a project-finance perspective and convert technical and contractual gaps into quantifiable risks, lender questions and required protections.

03

Analysis completed

  • Reconciled modelled export capacity and energy with the proposed connection point and interconnection scope.
  • Reviewed substation, line, metering, loss-factor and use-of-system assumptions.
  • Mapped planned and forced outage risk, dispatch instructions and network-related interruptions.
  • Distinguished compensated curtailment, uncompensated curtailment, deemed energy and force-majeure treatment.
  • Linked curtailment and delay scenarios to revenue, CFADS, DSCR and covenant headroom.
  • Prepared questions for connection, wheeling, PPA, EPC and lender documentation.

04

Decision value

The review prevented the model from treating grid access as a binary assumption. It showed how connection delay, network losses and curtailment allocation could alter debt capacity and the project’s ability to meet its PPA and financing obligations.

Public case-study boundary

The case is anonymised and development-stage. The stated connection basis was a modelling and study assumption, not evidence of final grid approval or guaranteed network capacity.
