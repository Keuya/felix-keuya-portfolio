Standard applied to every case

Authenticity comes from showing the limits

  • State whether the work was live, pilot, representative or internal.
  • Identify Felix’s personal contribution rather than claiming the whole transaction.
  • Use exact figures only when supported and disclose why they may have changed.
  • Show the uncomfortable question, not only the deliverable list.
  • State what remained unresolved and which professional approval was still required.
Working rule

A potential client should be able to disagree with the conclusion and still see that the analysis was built by someone who understands where the transaction can break.
