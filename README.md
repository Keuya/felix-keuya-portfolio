# Felix Keuya portfolio

Personal portfolio site for Felix Keuya, a renewable energy project finance and market intelligence analyst focused on African power markets.

The site now includes:

- project finance and market intelligence experience;
- white-label analyst services for developers, advisory teams and energy platforms;
- Renewables Rising research and editorial experience;
- selected deal work, models, writing and credentials;
- a browser-friendly resume page that can be printed or saved as PDF.

Static HTML/CSS, deployed on Vercel.
