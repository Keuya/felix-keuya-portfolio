What happens after an enquiry

The first output is clarity on fit, not a premature promise.

A submitted brief is reviewed for the decision required, client and project context, deadline, available information, conflicts, confidentiality and the level of specialist input required. Where legal, tax, engineering, environmental or assurance conclusions fall outside the agreed analytical role, that boundary is stated before work begins.

The engagement then moves through documented gates. The client knows what is included, what information is required, how assumptions are treated, when review is needed and what will be issued at the end.

Before work starts

A written scope confirms deliverables, fee, timing, assumptions, exclusions, review points, payment terms and client responsibilities.

Delivery workflow

Seven gates from project brief to final handover

The process is proportionate to the assignment. A short opportunity screen remains lean; a model review or lender pack carries deeper evidence and QA controls.

Qualification

Fit, decision and conflicts

Confirm what decision is being supported and whether the assignment can be accepted responsibly.

  • Client, project and counterparty check
  • Deadline and budget fit
  • Confidentiality and conflict review
Commercial

Scope and statement of work

Translate the brief into precise outputs and boundaries before substantive analysis begins.

  • Deliverables and acceptance criteria
  • Fee, timing and payment milestones
  • Assumptions, exclusions and revision limit
Mobilisation

Project setup and data readiness

Create the controlled workspace and identify evidence gaps before drawing conclusions.

  • Engagement number and version register
  • Data request and source log
  • Assumption and issue registers
Execution

Analysis by service-specific SOP

Apply the relevant workflow for financial models, PPA and wheeling, grid risk, IC materials or market diligence.

  • Reported facts separated from assumptions
  • Calculations and sources traceable
  • Material gaps escalated early
Quality

QA and human approval

Run calculation, evidence, commercial logic, confidentiality and presentation checks.

  • Model checks and output reconciliation
  • Source and wording review
  • Felix approval before external release
Client review

Controlled draft and comments

Issue a dated draft with unresolved matters visible and a defined review route.

  • Consolidated comments
  • New information assessed separately
  • Out-of-scope changes controlled
Closeout

Final files and handover

Issue the approved files, record limitations and close the assignment cleanly.

  • Final model, memo, pack or report
  • Issue register and action list
  • Archive, follow-up and permission controls

Human review layer

Automation accelerates work. It does not approve the message or conclusion.

Research tools, AI and code may support structuring, calculations, comparisons and drafting. They do not release client communications, approve a fee, certify a source, make a legal or engineering conclusion, or sign off a recommendation. Those points require deliberate human review.

MESSAGINGRecipient, facts, tone, commitments and confidentiality checked before sending.
CRITICAL INPUTSTariffs, CAPEX, debt terms, yield, dates and contract assumptions traced or labelled.
RECOMMENDATIONSEvidence, downside cases, limitations and decision logic reviewed by Felix.
FINAL FILESVersion, status, model checks, attachments and reliance wording confirmed.
