Synthetic public case

500 kW REC readiness and commercial model

A renewable-energy platform needed a repeatable way to decide whether an asset was commercially worth onboarding, technically ready for certificate issuance and capable of supporting defensible project-finance treatment.

01

Executive recommendation

Proceed conditionally. The synthetic asset passes the commercial screen, but should not be submitted until environmental-attribute ownership, no-double-claim controls, issuer meter acceptance, calibration evidence and the monthly data protocol are closed. Uncontracted REC revenue should remain equity upside rather than lender-recognised CFADS.

02

Problem addressed

A spreadsheet estimating REC revenue does not resolve whether the certificates can be issued, transferred and sold without a later ownership or data dispute. The model was therefore designed upstream of registry onboarding to combine legal rights, metering, data quality, commercial value and financing judgement in one go or no-go screen.

03

What the model delivers

Executive decision screenOne-page view of readiness, value, DSCR, recommendation and immediate blockers.
Monthly generation reconciliationRenewable and non-renewable output, common-bus energy, data gaps, eligibility haircuts, issued and sold RECs.
Project-finance overlayElectricity cash flow, debt schedule, CFADS, DSCR, equity cash flow and lender recognition of REC revenue.
REC readiness scorecardWeighted legal, metering, data, registry and market readiness with hard-gate controls.
Commercial scenariosLow, base, high and contracted price cases, plus price-versus-eligibility sensitivity.
Execution systemRisk register, evidence request, owners, dates and the decision unlocked by each action.

04

Readiness findings

51.3%

Legal and rights

Primary blockers were attribute clauses and the no-double-claim position. Required response: complete the contract matrix and signed claims memorandum.

74.3%

Technical and metering

Issuer meter acceptance and calibration evidence required validation of the SLD boundary, meter class, serials and certificates.

73.3%

Data and verification

Month-close and exception controls required a raw-data archive, reconciliation and approval workflow.

95.0%

Registry and market

The strongest workstream still required confirmation of local fees and binding buyer terms.

05

Commercial and financing judgement

The base case produced positive standalone REC value and a five-year owner cash uplift, but this did not improve minimum DSCR because uncontracted REC cash received zero lender recognition. The core judgement was that positive NPV is not the same as bankable revenue.

Five-year gross REC revenueUS$26,721
Five-year owner net REC cashUS$17,469
REC NPVUS$12,226
Equity IRR without REC22.7%

Unless the revenue is supported by durable contracting, clear title, enforceable assignment and a creditworthy buyer, the prudent financing case is to exclude it from CFADS and treat it as potential equity upside.

06

Execution plan

  • Confirm issuer process and local fees.
  • Validate the renewable and non-renewable meter boundary through an as-built single-line diagram.
  • Complete the environmental-attribute contract matrix.
  • Approve the no-double-claim and beneficiary memorandum.
  • Close calibration, seal and clock-synchronisation evidence.
  • Approve the monthly data and exception protocol.
  • Obtain comparable buyer or broker terms.
  • Issue a final investment screen after the hard gates close.

07

Decision value

The output showed whether the asset was worth onboarding, which EPC and contract defects could block registration, how hybrid generation should be reconciled and how REC cash should be treated in a lender or equity case.

Public case-study boundary
