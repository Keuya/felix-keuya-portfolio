Evidence

Not every project fact has the same certainty

I separate what is confirmed from what is still an assumption. This prevents a draft PPA, estimated project cost and target date from appearing equally certain.

  • Reported fact: supported by a current source.
  • Draft: useful for analysis, but not treated as signed.
  • Client assumption: used clearly and tested where material.
  • Analyst estimate: used for screening and labelled as an estimate.
  • Open item: no positive conclusion until evidence is available.

DRC hybrid example

Who carries the diesel cost?

SPV buys diesel
Fuel enters project cost and affects project cash flow.
Buyer bears diesel
Fuel is outside SPV cost and diesel energy should not inflate project revenue.
Buyer supplies diesel
Supply risk sits outside the SPV but still affects service performance.
More battery

Less battery
