# Keuya & Co. Brand Guide

## Brand architecture

**Primary brand:** Keuya & Co.

**Descriptor:** Energy & Infrastructure Advisory

**Founder attribution:** Founded by Felix Keuya

**Current positioning:** Independent renewable energy project finance, commercial diligence and strategic analyst support for African and emerging power markets.

Use **Keuya & Co.** as the outward-facing firm brand. Keep **Felix Keuya** visible as the founder and lead analyst in biographies, proposals, case studies and professional profiles.

Do not use **Keuya & Partners** until the business has genuine partners. The future transition can be made without changing the core Keuya name.

Blue should carry trust, finance and institutional depth. Green should signal energy transition and progress. Use green selectively for actions, highlights and data points rather than as a full-page background.

## Typography

- **Display and headings:** Fraunces
- **Body, navigation and data:** Inter
- Use sentence case for headings.
- Avoid excessive all-caps. Reserve uppercase for small labels, categories and document references.

## Wordmark

The standard wordmark is **Keuya & Co.** paired with a restrained `K` monogram. The website uses the wordmark without claiming a partnership structure.

Preferred lock-up:

**Keuya & Co.**  
*Energy & Infrastructure Advisory*

## Photography

- Use high-resolution, real photography of renewable-energy assets, transmission infrastructure, industrial sites and professional working environments.
- Keep portraits natural, sharply focused and professionally cropped.
- Prefer neutral or dark-blue backgrounds and restrained grading.
- Avoid generic AI finance imagery, glowing maps, handshakes, coins, laptops and futuristic grids.

## Tone

Write like an advisory firm: clear, evidence-led, commercially aware and direct. Emphasise the decision being supported, the evidence used, the material risks and the output delivered.

## Application hierarchy

1. Website and proposals: **Keuya & Co.** as the masthead; Felix Keuya as founder and lead analyst.
2. LinkedIn profile: Felix Keuya remains the personal profile name; banner and featured assets use Keuya & Co.
3. Email signature: Felix Keuya, Founder and Lead Analyst, Keuya & Co.
4. CV: retain Felix Keuya as the candidate name; use Keuya & Co. only for independent advisory work where factually accurate.
5. Reports and models: Keuya & Co. wordmark in the header or cover, with the descriptor beneath it.
