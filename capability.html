Capability summary

Extra analytical capacity for renewable-energy decisions that have to survive scrutiny.

Felix supports developers, investors, lenders, advisers and energy platforms with financial models, contract-linked commercial analysis, investment materials and African power-market diligence. The work is delivered under a defined scope, with evidence states and human approval before external release.

Where Felix fits

Between a busy project team and the decision it still needs to make

Assignments typically start when a team has a model, data room or market opportunity but lacks the time to reconcile the technical, commercial, financing and contract assumptions into one defensible answer.

Felix’s role is not to replace legal, technical, tax or lender advisers. It is to make the analytical gaps visible, trace their impact into the numbers and prepare the material those specialists and decision-makers need.

Delivery and human review

Tools accelerate the work; Felix approves what is relied upon

  • Scope, fee, timing and exclusions are confirmed in writing.
  • Material inputs are classified by source and evidence status.
  • Models are reconciled to the written recommendation.
  • Critical claims, client messages and attachments are reviewed by Felix.
  • Specialist legal, tax, engineering or assurance conclusions are not implied.

Typical engagement sequence
