58.5 MWdc / 50 MWac Zambia solar IPP financial model and lender review

The assignment connected energy yield, PPA revenue, EPC and development costs, debt capacity, reserve accounts and contractual risk into one lender-style finance case and a prioritised bankability workplan.

04

Base-case credit and return view

MetricCase outputReview interpretation
Project usesApprox. US$59.4mRequired reconciliation to final EPC, connection and development budgets
Debt frameworkUp to 75% debt capFinal sizing remained subject to lender terms and downside headroom
Minimum DSCRApprox. 1.20xMeets the modelled floor but leaves limited base-case buffer
LLCRApprox. 1.47xProvides stronger life-of-loan cover than the minimum annual period
DSRASix months of forward debt serviceLiquidity protection for temporary cash-flow disruption
Equity IRRApprox. 15.0%Aligned with the modelled equity hurdle, not a guaranteed sponsor return
Credit conclusion: the model supported a lender-compliant base case, but the minimum DSCR sat close to the threshold. Final debt capacity therefore depended as much on contractual protection and downside cases as on the headline base-case return.

06

Bankability questions produced from the model

  • Who bears curtailment, network outage and wheeling interruption risk, and when does deemed energy apply?
  • Is payment security funded, on demand, replenishable and accessible to lenders?
  • Do termination payments cover debt, break costs, equity and transfer obligations under the relevant events?
  • Are tariff indexation, taxes, balancing, trading, wheeling and agent charges fully reflected in net project revenue?
  • Can the EPC price, completion date, performance guarantees and liquidated damages support the modelled construction case?
  • Are DSRA funding, distribution lock-up, cure rights and lender step-in provisions consistent across the model and agreements?

08

Decision value

The work did not treat a positive IRR as the end of the analysis. It identified the assumptions carrying the credit case, showed where lender headroom was thin and converted model weaknesses into a focused list of contractual, technical and financing actions.
