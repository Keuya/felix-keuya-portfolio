Short biography

50-word bio

Felix Keuya is a Nairobi-based renewable energy project finance analyst working across financial modelling, project screening, PPA and wheeling analysis, market diligence and African power markets. His work sits between engineering, finance and commercial decision-making, with a focus on solar PV, battery storage and privately financed power projects.

Longer bio

Felix Keuya works on renewable energy project finance and commercial analysis across African power markets. He combines an engineering foundation with financial modelling, debt sizing, PPA analysis, market diligence and investment decision support. His public work covers utility-scale solar, battery energy storage, corporate PPAs and wheeling, grid and curtailment risk, offtaker credit and project screening. He also provides fixed-scope and white-label analyst support to developers, investors, lenders, advisers and energy platforms.
