Public basis

Official framework used to structure the representative case

The public model reflects the structure described in current official material: time-of-use matching, generator and buyer agreements, use-of-system and administration charges, loss-adjusted credits, connection requirements and monthly settlement. All prices and financial outputs in the workbook remain illustrative.

Live mandate

What a transaction-specific wheeling case adds

  • Half-hourly or monthly buyer load, generation and profile-matching analysis.
  • Actual Eskom or municipality-specific network tariffs, loss factors and administration fees.
  • Connection, use-of-system, account amendment, metering and settlement agreement review.
  • Volume, shape, take-or-pay, shortfall, surplus energy and imbalance allocation.
  • Curtailment, deemed energy, grid outage, change-in-law and termination-payment scenarios.
  • Buyer and generator credit support, security deposits, guarantees and payment timelines.
  • Generator project-finance model, buyer savings model and a reconciled commercial term sheet.
