01

The bridge between the model and the machine

I trained as a mechanical and energy engineer at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, then moved into investment analysis. That order matters. The finance toolkit is the lender's: CFADS, cover ratios, repayment sculpting, reserve accounts, covenant headroom and downside cases. The engineering foundation means yield, degradation, availability and O&M assumptions get challenged before the spreadsheet does.

Alongside deal work I build market intelligence on African energy: deal and policy databases, solar manufacturing and supply chain mapping, regional power pool analysis, and a running read on renewables news across the continent.

Final year engineering project: design and techno economic assessment of an Organic Rankine Cycle system recovering roughly 15 MW of thermal energy from cement manufacturing, from thermal integration to cost drivers and adoption barriers.

03

Featured publication

Africa Solar Manufacturing Report

In partnership with Renewables Rising

Contributing author on a continent wide study of Africa's emerging solar manufacturing base, produced with Renewables Rising. The report maps capacity, announcements and investment across modules, cells, wafers, solar glass, inverters and storage assembly, and weighs localisation policy, demand growth and supply chain risk to identify where manufacturing can genuinely take root.

The underlying research database standardises project status, capacity and investment data with full source traceability, and now supports procurement, expansion and investment diligence work.
