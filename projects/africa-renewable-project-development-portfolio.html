03

Consistent portfolio review method

01

Define the claim

Capacity, location, stage, offtaker, tariff, cost, connection and target timetable.

02

Grade the evidence

Separate executed documents, draft studies, sponsor assumptions, estimates and research gaps.

03

Test the market

Demand, procurement route, regulation, grid, offtaker and financing appetite.

04

Test the economics

Revenue, CAPEX, OPEX, debt capacity, returns and sensitivities.

05

Map development gaps

Land, permits, studies, EPC, grid, PPA, security package and funding.

06

Recommend the next gate

Advance, pause, rework or reject, with actions and evidence owners.

04

Evidence and status discipline

StatusMeaning in the analysisPermitted conclusion
Reported factSupported by a current primary or authoritative sourceCan be presented as evidence with source and date
Draft / under negotiationDocument exists but is not executed or finalCan inform analysis but remains a closing condition or risk
Model assumptionValue selected for calculation pending confirmationMust be disclosed and sensitivity-tested
EstimateDerived from comparable data or incomplete informationUseful for screening, not presented as a reported fact
Research pendingMaterial evidence has not yet been found or suppliedNo positive conclusion until the gap is closed
Not publicly disclosedInformation may exist privately but is not in the available evidenceRequest from sponsor or counterparty before reliance
Why this matters: development portfolios often contain confident headlines but uneven supporting evidence. The review protects the client from confusing an announced opportunity, a sponsor assumption and a financeable project.

06

DRC hybrid-power scenario work

The mine-linked case required more than a single solar forecast. Alternative PV, BESS and diesel configurations were separated so the commercial model could show who supplied fuel, who paid for diesel generation and whether diesel-linked energy belonged in project revenue.

Configuration issueModel treatmentCommercial question
PV capacityAlternative 45 MW and 68 MW casesWhich design best matches load, land, grid and capital constraints?
BESS size100 MWh to 210 MWh storage casesWhat service, duration and dispatch value justifies the battery cost?
Diesel supportSeparate fuel, operating and energy assumptionsDoes the SPV, offtaker or third party bear diesel cost and supply risk?
RevenueExclude diesel energy where the offtaker supplies or bears fuelWhich energy is contractually payable to the project company?
Debt serviceScenario-specific CFADS and DSCRWhich configuration remains financeable under operational downside?

07

Portfolio prioritisation output

Advance

Evidence supports the next gate

Material development rights, market logic and economics are sufficiently supported to justify deeper diligence or capital.

Rework

Opportunity remains credible but incomplete

Specific commercial, technical, contractual or financing assumptions must be corrected before progression.

Pause

Evidence is below the decision threshold

Further spend should wait until ownership, offtake, grid, permits or economics are materially better supported.

09

Decision value

The portfolio analysis made projects comparable without pretending they were equally mature. It exposed unsupported claims, prioritised the developments with the strongest evidence and economics, and translated research and model gaps into a practical workplan for management, investors or lenders.
