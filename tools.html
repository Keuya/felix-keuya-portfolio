When to stop using the calculator

Once the answer depends on timing, contracts or downside cases.

A quick calculator is useful for screening. It is not a substitute for a project model when cash flow changes by month or year, debt is sculpted, tax and reserve accounts matter, or the PPA has detailed settlement rules.

If the first-pass result looks interesting, move into the underlying model and documents. That is where the financing and commercial risks become visible.
