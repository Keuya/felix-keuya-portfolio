Independent investment case

Investment committee memo and valuation for 120 MW solar PV plus six-hour BESS

The case combines a project-finance model with an investment-committee paper so the decision is based on the assumptions, downside resilience and conditions behind the returns, not the headline IRR alone.

01

The committee question

Should capital be advanced to the project on the current terms, reworked to improve the risk-adjusted return, or paused until the storage, revenue and financing assumptions are better supported?

07

Decision conditions produced

  • Validate the solar resource, battery dispatch assumptions, cycle profile and performance degradation through independent technical evidence.
  • Confirm that the PPA pays for the services and energy actually required from the storage system.
  • Reconcile the EPC price with battery augmentation, replacement, warranty and contingency assumptions.
  • Test debt sizing against conservative generation, efficiency, CAPEX and delay cases rather than the base case alone.
  • Align performance guarantees, availability, liquidated damages and termination payments with the model.
  • Retain sufficient reserve and sponsor support for replacement, overrun and temporary operating underperformance.

Decision value
