Independent investment case

Investment committee memo and valuation for 120 MW solar PV plus six-hour BESS

A utility-scale solar and storage opportunity required a model and decision paper that linked technical design, contracted revenue, financing structure and downside resilience to an invest-or-rework recommendation.

Case classificationIndependent analytical model and decision memo

The page demonstrates the analysis and output structure. It is not a regulated investment recommendation or evidence of completed financing.

Asset120 MW solar PV + 6-hour BESS
PPA case8.8 USc/kWh
CAPEXUS$184m
Equity caseUS$62m
Analysed DSCR1.57x
Project IRR10.37%

01

Situation

The investment case depended on how storage design, charging logic, availability, degradation and replacement assumptions translated into contracted energy, operating cost and debt service.

02

Mandate

Build a FAST-style project model, value the project and prepare an investment-committee narrative that separated headline returns from the risks capable of changing the decision.

03

Analysis completed

  • Built project and equity cash flows from generation, PPA, storage, CAPEX, OPEX, tax and financing assumptions.
  • Calculated project IRR, equity returns, CFADS, debt service and DSCR.
  • Tested tariff, yield, storage performance, CAPEX, delay and operating-cost sensitivities.
  • Reviewed whether the revenue structure compensated for storage duration, degradation and replacement risk.
  • Prepared an investment thesis, key risks, mitigants, valuation observations and conditions to proceed.
  • Converted model outputs into a concise recommendation rather than presenting calculations without judgement.

04

Decision value

The combined model and memo made clear which assumptions drove the 10.37% project return, where debt-service resilience came from and what commercial revisions would be required to improve the investment case.
