Delivery standards

Built for scrutiny and efficient handover

Tools support the work, but critical facts, client messages and conclusions remain subject to human review.

Transparent analysis

Inputs, calculations, outputs and checks are separated. Facts, assumptions, estimates and judgements are distinguished.

Controlled evidence

Material values carry a source, date and confidence basis where the assignment requires external research.

Human approval

Felix reviews scope, material assumptions, client messaging, recommendations and final attachments before release.

Confidential delivery

NDA, controlled folders, versioning, limited sharing and client-authorised case-study use.

Usable outputs

Excel, Word, PDF and PowerPoint files designed for the client's decision process and templates.

Clear limitations

No unsupported lender approval, legal conclusion, tax opinion, engineering sign-off or assurance claim.

Relevant assignment

Share the decision, available files and deadline.

Fit, information gaps and the proposed scope are confirmed before substantive work begins.
