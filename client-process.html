Engagement workflow

What happens after a brief is received

No substantive analysis starts until scope, confidentiality and the information basis are clear.

01 · Qualification

Confirm fit and the decision required

Review project stage, audience, confidentiality, deadline, budget, conflicts and whether specialist legal, tax or engineering advice is required.

02 · Scope

Agree deliverables and boundaries

Issue a written scope covering outputs, fee, timing, assumptions, exclusions, revision limits, client responsibilities and payment terms.

03 · Readiness

Log evidence and missing information

Classify critical inputs as reported facts, client-provided facts, assumptions, estimates or research pending. Material gaps are raised rather than silently filled.

04 · Analysis

Execute the relevant service workflow

Build or review the model, test sensitivities, analyse contracts and market evidence, maintain source and issue registers, and prepare the agreed decision material.

05 · Human QA

Review calculations, claims and wording

Felix checks formulas, source traceability, recommendation logic, caveats, confidentiality, version control and every client-facing message before release.

06 · Delivery

Issue, revise and close

Send a controlled draft, resolve the agreed review round, issue final files and a handover note, then archive evidence and request feedback or referral.

Human review gates

What technology does not decide

AI and automation support speed and consistency; they do not replace judgement or accountability.
