07

Common questions

Can the work be delivered under our company brand?

Yes. I can work from your templates, naming conventions and review process. The engagement can remain confidential and the final output can carry your brand.

Will you sign an NDA before receiving documents?

Yes. Do not send confidential models, contracts or data room material through the public form. I can review and sign a reasonable mutual NDA first.

Do you replace legal, tax, technical or investment advisers?

No. I provide analytical and execution support. Legal, tax, independent engineering and regulated investment advice should remain with appropriately qualified advisers.

Can you work with an existing model or memo template?

Yes. Existing files are often the fastest starting point. I can review, update, restructure or produce a clean parallel analysis depending on the assignment.

How are fees and revisions handled?

Each proposal states the fee, payment schedule, deliverables, assumptions, exclusions and included revision round. Additional scope is agreed before it is started.
