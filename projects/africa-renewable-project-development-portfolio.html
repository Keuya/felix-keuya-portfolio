01

Situation

The portfolio included development-stage assets ranging from a Zambia private-offtake solar IPP to a DRC solar-plus-storage study and West African utility-scale solar opportunities. The evidence and project maturity varied, so analysis had to distinguish current facts, model assumptions and unresolved development work.

02

Mandate

Support project screening, model review, feasibility documentation, market diligence, grid and offtake analysis and investor-facing materials across the portfolio without overstating project status.

03

Project exposure and analysis

  • Zambia: supported a 58.5 MWdc / 50 MWac solar IPP finance case covering PPA revenue, debt sizing, lender metrics, grid connection, wheeling and payment security.
  • DRC: reviewed a 100 MW solar-plus-BESS development study and mine-linked hybrid-power analysis, including alternative PV, storage and diesel configurations and fuel-risk allocation.
  • Côte d’Ivoire: reviewed a 52.4 MWdc / 47.3 MWac solar project with a proposed 90 kV connection, long-term utility PPA and approximately US$58.4m project cost.
  • Guinea: project exposure included a 100 MW solar opportunity in Boké and a 5 MW project in Kouroussa.
  • Senegal, Niger and Mauritania: portfolio materials covered 100 MW Kédougou, 100 MW Gorou Banda, 100 MW Gazaoua and 60 MW Nouakchott opportunities at early development or pre-feasibility stages.
  • Across markets: separated reported facts from development assumptions and linked regulation, grid, offtaker, EPC and financing risks to the next diligence step.

04

Decision value

The portfolio work created a comparable view across projects, exposed where development claims were ahead of supporting evidence and helped prioritise the workstreams most relevant to bankability and investor readiness.
