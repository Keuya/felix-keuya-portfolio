Mining power opportunity screen

Solar IPP · Southern Africa

Solar model and commercial review

ScaleAbout 50 MWac
FocusNet tariff and debt capacity
ReviewModel, PPA and grid terms

Brief

A utility solar project needed a financing view that reflected the full contract chain, not only the headline PPA tariff.

What I did

  • Updated energy, cost, tariff and debt assumptions
  • Reviewed CFADS, DSCR, LLCR, reserves and sponsor returns
  • Tested wheeling, losses, service fees, payment security and curtailment

What mattered

A stronger model return did not remove the open contract risks. The financing case depended on the cash that remained after every service and grid deduction.

Grid and revenue risk

Connection and curtailment analysis

QuestionWhich MWh become paid energy?
InputsGrid, meter and contract terms
ImpactRevenue and debt service

Brief

The generation case needed to distinguish technical output from the electricity that could leave the site, be measured and be paid for.

What I did

  • Separated gross, available, delivered and paid energy
  • Mapped outages, export limits, losses and curtailment to revenue
  • Linked each risk to the responsible contract and financial remedy

What mattered

A single energy haircut hides the reason cash is lost. The model is more useful when each missing MWh has an owner and a remedy.

120 MW solar plus BESS model

PPA case8.8 USc per kWh
CAPEXUS$184m
Modelled DSCR1.57x

Brief

A representative investment case tested how a six hour battery changed revenue, costs, debt capacity and returns.

What I did
