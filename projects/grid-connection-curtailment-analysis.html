Anonymised development-stage assignment

Grid connection, wheeling and curtailment review for a 50 MWac solar IPP

The review tested whether the energy assumed in the financial model could physically reach the offtaker, be metered and settled, and remain compensated under grid outages, losses and curtailment.

01

The bankability question

What portion of modelled generation can be exported, delivered and paid for, and who bears the financial impact when the connection is delayed, the network is unavailable or dispatch is restricted?

05

Quantification approach

  • Reconciled AC capacity, generation and export assumptions with the proposed connection capacity.
  • Applied availability, degradation, losses and curtailment as separate model lines rather than one blended haircut.
  • Tested connection delay through lost revenue, additional development cost, financing fees and IDC.
  • Ran curtailment and grid-outage cases through revenue, CFADS, DSCR, LLCR and distributions.
  • Separated compensated deemed energy from uncompensated curtailed energy.
  • Mapped each material model assumption to the agreement expected to support it.

06

Conditions required before reliance

Technical

Approved connection basis

Capacity, voltage, network works, grid code, metering and energisation responsibilities confirmed.

Commercial

Coherent contract allocation

PPA, wheeling, connection and EPC documents use consistent delivery, outage and curtailment concepts.

Finance

Downside-resilient debt case

Debt sizing and reserves remain acceptable under quantified delay, loss and curtailment cases.

08

Decision value

The review converted “grid access” from a yes-or-no assumption into a quantified set of risks, responsibilities and protections. This allowed the financing case to distinguish energy that could be generated from energy that could reliably be delivered and monetised.

Public case-study boundary

The case is anonymised and development-stage. The connection basis was drawn from studies and model assumptions, not a public statement of final grid approval, guaranteed capacity or completed network works.
