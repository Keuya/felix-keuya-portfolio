What potential clients should be able to judge

Not whether the pages look polished. Whether the analysis is useful.

  • Was the decision framed correctly?
  • Were facts separated from assumptions and estimates?
  • Did the analysis expose what could break the transaction?
  • Were contracts, grid and counterparties linked back to cash flow?
  • Did the output tell a principal what to do next?
Working standard

A clean model is not enough. The real test is whether the work makes an uncomfortable project question visible early enough to change the decision.
