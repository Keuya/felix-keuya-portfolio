What I do

Analysis for decisions, not just documents.

My work sits between engineering, commercial analysis and project finance. I help project teams test whether the revenue, cost, debt, grid and contract assumptions in a renewable energy opportunity fit together well enough to support the next decision.

That can mean reviewing a financial model before lender discussions, testing PPA and wheeling economics, screening a new market, building a decision memo or providing recurring white-label analyst capacity to a lean team.

Working style

Useful to developers, investors, lenders and advisers.

  • Start with the commercial or investment decision that needs to be made.
  • Separate verified inputs from assumptions and open questions.
  • Connect technical assumptions to revenue, debt service and returns.
  • Show downside cases rather than relying on a single base case.
  • Keep outputs concise enough for management, lender or investment review.
