Where I help

Turn project information into a finance decision.

I focus on the questions that change whether a renewable energy project is commercially credible and financeable: revenue quality, tariff assumptions, CAPEX and OPEX, debt capacity, cash available for debt service, cover ratios, reserve accounts, downside resilience and risk allocation.

The work is designed for live project teams that need a clear view before committing more development time, taking a case to management, approaching lenders or responding to investor questions.

Questions

Before we start

Can you work with confidential project files?

Yes. An NDA can be agreed before private models, contracts or diligence files are shared.

Do you replace legal, tax or independent engineering advisers?

No. My role is financial, commercial and market analysis. Formal legal, tax and independent engineering opinions remain with the relevant licensed or qualified advisers.

Can the work be one-off or ongoing?

Both. Project screens and model reviews are usually fixed-scope assignments; recurring analyst capacity can be set up as a monthly engagement.
