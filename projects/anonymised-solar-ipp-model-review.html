Material findings

Issues are prioritised by their effect on the decision.

The public issue register includes critical, high, medium and low-priority findings rather than an unstructured list of comments.

Model integrity

Check circularity, debt-service logic, terminal treatment, reserve movements and consistency across outputs.

Revenue evidence

Confirm tariff, indexation, yield, availability, degradation, curtailment and settlement assumptions.

Financing terms

Verify debt quantum, tenor, sculpting, interest, DSRA, covenants, lock-up and distribution conditions.

Contract bankability

Test payment security, termination compensation, change in law, grid risk and lender access to remedies.

Source control

Label each material input as verified, client-provided, analyst estimate or unresolved.

Release control

Do not issue the conclusion until the figures, wording, limitations and attachments have been human reviewed.
