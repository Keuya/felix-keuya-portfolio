Sub-Saharan Africa renewable energy opportunity screen

A private-capital audience needed a concise way to translate African industrial-power themes into specific opportunities, investment questions and evidence requirements before committing resources to full diligence.

01

Situation

The audience had substantial energy-sector experience but needed a renewable-energy entry screen grounded in industrial demand, private offtake, grid constraints and project-finance discipline. A broad market report would not be enough; the pack had to show where an investable question might exist and what evidence was still missing.

02

Mandate

Convert a regional investment thesis into a short, decision-focused opportunity screen suitable for management discussion. The work had to connect market signals to project archetypes, explain the commercial logic and establish clear diligence gates before a model build or investment-committee process.

03

Pack structure and analysis

  • Framed the investment thesis around industrial demand, private power procurement and transmission constraints.
  • Translated familiar oil-and-gas concepts into renewable equivalents, including resource rights, PPA offtake, direct-wire infrastructure, yield certification and political-risk cover.
  • Developed four opportunity archetypes rather than presenting an undifferentiated country list.
  • Used a six-lens bankability screen covering offtake, grid, tariff and FX, regulation, debt capacity and downside resilience.
  • Separated market signals, indicative ranges, assumptions and outstanding evidence.
  • Ended with a practical next-step discussion covering geography, ticket size, risk limits, return threshold and deal-flow cadence.

04

Opportunity archetypes

DRC Copperbelt

Concept: mine-tethered solar plus BESS, 20–80 MW, using a direct-wire or dedicated supply structure.

Critical tests: confirmed expansion load, corporate credit, grid dependency, direct-wire rights, security package, FX and political-risk mitigation.

Zimbabwe processing cluster

Concept: dedicated industrial solar hybrid, 30–120 MW, with private PPA and hybrid supply.

Critical tests: processing-load timing, hard-currency revenue, licensing, grid fallback, dispatch, tariff indexation and convertibility.

Guinea industrial corridor

Concept: pre-refinery power infrastructure, 50–200 MW, developed around anchor load and staged expansion.

Critical tests: refinery FID, connection rights, phased capacity, anchor-customer credit, construction sequencing and sovereign interfaces.

Tanzania graphite coast

Concept: solar and storage cluster or island grid, 15–60 MW, serving multiple industrial users.

Critical tests: clustered load, customer diversification, storage and backup optimisation, land, licences, metering and common-infrastructure governance.

05

Six-lens bankability screen

01

Offtake quality

Buyer credit, payment security, parent support, LC, escrow and termination protection.

02

Grid and curtailment

Connection route, capacity, metering, losses, dispatch priority, outages and risk allocation.

03

Tariff and FX

Delivered-cost bridge, indexation, currency, convertibility and stressed coverage.

04

Regulatory readiness

Generation, trading, land, environmental, interconnection and construction approvals.

05

Debt capacity

Realistic leverage, DSCR, LLCR, tenor, grace, sculpting, DSRA and lender appetite.

06

Downside resilience

Yield, delay, CAPEX, tariff, curtailment, payment delay and interest-rate stress.

06

Decision value

The pack turned a regional theme into a controlled discussion: which archetype justified deeper work, what would have to be true, which documents were needed next and which issues could stop the opportunity before further advisory cost was incurred.
