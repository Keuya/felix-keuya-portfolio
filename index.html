The technical-commercial bridge

I come from a mechanical engineering background (BSc, Dedan Kimathi University of Technology) and moved into investment analysis: testing assumptions, identifying risks, understanding regulation, and translating energy-sector complexity into clear commercial decisions.

My working toolkit is the lender's: CFADS, DSCR/LLCR/PLCR, repayment sculpting, reserve accounts, cash waterfalls, covenant headroom and downside cases. The engineering foundation means the model assumptions — yield, degradation, availability, O&M — get challenged before the finance does.

Alongside deal work I build market intelligence on African energy: deal databases, solar manufacturing and supply-chain mapping, EAPP/SAPP market development, and a weekly read on renewables news across the continent.
